## Purpose

Integrate the entire course: the learner conducts a guided budget investigation on a chosen sector and produces a one-page budget investigation brief.

## Capstone sectors

Education, Healthcare, Agriculture, Public transportation, Social protection, Flood control, Local infrastructure.

## Learning objectives

After this module, the learner can:

1. Execute the eight-step investigation: define need → identify institution → find allocation → place in cycle → compare approved vs actual → examine accomplishments → identify issue → propose action.
2. Apply document skills (Module 7) and evaluation criteria (Module 8) to real or sample data.
3. Produce a one-page brief that states the issue, the evidence, and a decision-ready recommendation.

## Investigation steps

1. Define the public need.
2. Identify the responsible institution.
3. Find the relevant budget allocation.
4. Determine its stage in the budget cycle.
5. Compare approved and actual expenditure.
6. Examine physical accomplishments.
7. Identify an implementation or accountability issue.
8. Propose a question, recommendation, or participation action.

Each step maps to a module: 1→9.2, 2→9.3, 3→7.1, 4→4, 5→7.3, 6→5.3, 7→8, 8→9.4.

## Interactive element

* **int-09 Capstone investigation workbook:** multi-step form + brief generator. Guides the learner through the eight steps with prompts, hints linking back to the relevant lesson, and per-sector sample datasets (curated, dated); final screen renders a downloadable one-page brief (printable/PDF). Fallback: printable worksheet packet with the same eight steps and dataset sheets.

## Assessment

The brief is the assessment: a rubric (issue clarity, evidence use, cycle placement, recommendation quality) supports self-assessment; no graded scoring in MVP.

## Worked examples used

None new — the capstone is learner-generated. Per-sector sample datasets use only public budget data; the transport dataset may mirror the active-transport items used across Modules 4, 7, and 9.

## Sourcing notes

New authoring. Per-sector curated datasets are the main content dependency: each needs a fiscal-year snapshot of allocation, execution, and accomplishment figures with sources. Owner and review process per build spec open decisions.

## Open questions

* Dataset scope per sector: how many budget years deep? Two to three years recommended for the comparison steps.
* Should learners be able to investigate a sector outside the seven provided? MVP: no — free-form raises support burden; revisit post-MVP.
