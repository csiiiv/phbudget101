## Purpose

Orient the learner, set expectations, establish a baseline of prior knowledge, and route them onto a learning path.

## Learning objectives

After this module, the learner can:

1. Describe what the course covers and who it is for.
2. Choose a learning path appropriate to their goals.
3. Recall the two orienting questions that frame the whole course: where does government money come from, and where does it go?

## Lessons

### 00.1 Welcome to Budget 101

* Guiding question: What will I be able to do after this course?
* Concepts: course scope; the PFM-first framing (advocacy as the application of budget literacy); who the course serves; the three learning paths (quick overview, full course, topic-based reference)
* Interactive or visual: none (concept map comes in 00.2)
* Worked example: none
* Activity and knowledge check: none; ends with the path choice

### 00.2 Where the Money Comes From and Where It Goes

* Guiding question: Where does government money come from, and where does it go?
* Concepts: the course's two orienting questions; preview of the answer (revenue in → budget decisions → services out) at headline level only
* Interactive or visual: int-06 concept map
* Worked example: none
* Activity and knowledge check: concept-map exploration is the activity; no check

### 00.3 Find Your Starting Point

* Guiding question: What do I already know about the budget?
* Concepts: none; diagnostic purposes only
* Interactive or visual: int-07 diagnostic quiz
* Worked example: none
* Activity and knowledge check: the quiz is the activity; result screen recommends a path and entry module

## Interactive element

* **int-06 Concept map:** node-link diagram of the flow from revenues → budget process → services, with clickable nodes showing one-line previews and linking forward to the relevant module. Static data. Fallback: static diagram with module links.
* **int-07 Diagnostic quiz:** 6–10 lightweight items mapping to Modules 1–9 topic areas; results screen suggests quick-overview vs full-course and flags modules the learner may skip or review. No scoring beyond path advice. Fallback: three-question self-check list.

## Assessment

No graded assessment. The diagnostic (int-07) is advisory only and explicitly non-judgmental.

## Worked examples used

None. Orientation only.
