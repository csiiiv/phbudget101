## Public Financial Management, in one sentence

**Public Financial Management (PFM)** is the system by which government plans, directs, and controls its money to deliver public services.

Every public service you can name — a classroom, a vaccine, a road, a social worker's salary — has a budget behind it. The budget is how the government turns its promises into resources, and resources into services.

## The budget is the clearest statement of priorities

Governments say many things. The budget is where those statements meet arithmetic. When a program is announced but never funded, the announcement is not yet a policy. **Follow the money**: what is funded is what is real.

## Every peso travels a chain

A peso moves from revenue, through a budget decision, into a program, and out as a service. This course follows that chain module by module — starting in the next lesson with the four ways to read any budget.
